About

I am a PhD student in the ACiD (Algorithms and Complexity in Durham) group supervised by Dr Barnaby Martin and Professor Andrei Krokhin.

My main research area is the complexity of the Quantified Constraint Satisfaction Problem (QCSP) on graphs, focusing particularly on different kinds of QCSP problems restricted to different kinds of graphs.
